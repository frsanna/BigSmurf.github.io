About Me

I lead technical delivery. For fifteen years I have owned both sides of the work: the technical direction, and the organization required to take it into production. I still design and write the software. That is what keeps the direction credible.

The engagements cross industries: enterprise customers, and the large partners who deliver with them. The substance is custom integrations, customer-facing applications, and the technical decisions that hold a program together when scope and priority move. I am the technical voice with the customer. I train the engineering teams who will run what we deliver, including international groups, in hands-on workshops. After go-live I stay accountable for production.

Today I lead squads of three to six, on client work and on internal work. I set the architecture, the roadmap, and who delivers what, and I remain in review and implementation. I have also taken a product of my own from idea to release.
